# Ethics application outline (DRAFT, Week 4)

Built from the MACS ethics checklist, FAQ (v3) and datasets guidance. I have **not** seen the real ethics.hw.ac.uk form yet, so field names will differ. Use this to collect your answers, then paste them into the form.

Timeline: draft to supervisor by **Mon 19 Oct**; submit by **Wed 28 Oct** (hard deadline Fri 30 Oct). No work with participants before approval, and pilots count.

## 1. Upfront (title, description, work and evaluation)
- Title and short description (use the project-system text).
- What I will do, not just the aim: build 4 systems, a ~200 question test set, run evaluation, optional usability study.
- Describe the system users would test: a chat web page that answers university policy questions with sources, or escalates to staff. No personal data is needed to use it.

## 2. Route A: no human participants
- Data the system works with: public university documents (web pages, PDFs).
- Permission: the datasets guidance asks for authorisation when using organisation data and when scraping. State whose documents they are and that you have (or will have) permission. **Ask your supervisor who can confirm this.**
- Test questions written by me. If any are generated by an LLM, treat them as synthetic data: say which tool, that it is not scraping real data, that it is used only for this project and never shared.
- No personal data, no real student records, anonymous logs.

## 3. Route B: human involvement (include it so the option stays open)
| Item | What to write |
|---|---|
| Why participants are needed | Usability and trust assessment; peers to check question labels |
| Who and how many | HWUM students, about 10-15 for usability, 2-3 peers for labelling about 30 items |
| Recruitment | Class announcement or university channel. Confirm channel with supervisor |
| Duration | [to decide: e.g. about 30-40 min per session] |
| Information sheet | What data is collected, why, how stored, how to access it, right to stop at any time and have data removed |
| Consent | Form with typed full name, date and a consent statement. Never anonymous. Keep a copy |
| Vulnerability | Screening: age 18 or over and no significant mental or physical conditions (even for HWU-only recruits, as some may be 17 or need extra support) |
| Online tools | Only HWU-approved software for surveys and sessions |
| Stopping | Participants may stop at any point; their data is then removed |

## 4. Conversational-agent risks (FAQ item 8 says to address these)
- The bot may give a wrong or insensitive answer: show a disclaimer, show sources, escalate when unsure, use scripted tasks.
- Participants may type personal or sensitive details: tell them not to, prefer scripted questions, keep logs anonymous.
- **Third-party processing:** typed questions go to a hosted LLM API. The information sheet must say so, and the application must say what is sent and why this is acceptable.

## 5. Anonymisation and data
- State whether data is fully or pseudo-anonymised, and how participants can access or remove theirs.
- Storage location, retention, and what is uploaded at the end (only anonymised data).

## 6. Health and safety
- Standard software in an office or lab. No special equipment. Say so.

## 7. Documents to prepare
- Participant information sheet and consent form (templates are linked in the handbook).
- Sample questionnaire (usability scale plus trust rating). The checklist says the consent form and sample questionnaire go in the **D1 appendix**.
